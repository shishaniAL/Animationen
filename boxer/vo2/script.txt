I'm going to tell you something nobody understands about me.
I go to school every day.
But deep down, I know this isn't the life I want.
I don't dream about getting a normal job, working my entire life, and waiting for the weekend.
I want to build something of my own.
I want freedom. I want success. I want to become a millionaire.
But my parents don't understand.
Every time I talk about my dreams, they tell me to focus on school.
Sometimes they get angry. Sometimes they shout.
And honestly… it hurts.
Because all I want is for them to believe in me.
But maybe they don't need to believe in me right now.
Maybe I just need to keep working.
So while everyone thinks I'm wasting my time dreaming…
I'll be learning. Building. Failing. Trying again.
I don't need to prove anything with words.
One day, I want to walk into that same room…
Look at everyone who doubted me…
And finally say nothing.
Just smile.
Because the results will speak for themselves.
And that's when I'll let the silence do the talking.
