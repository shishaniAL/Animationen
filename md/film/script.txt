This is Mickel Dawson.
Mickel is 82 years old.
Every day, Mickel did exactly the same thing.
He woke up at 5 AM.
He ate the same oatmeal.
He solved the same crossword.
And at 7 PM, he was in bed.
Mickel was so boring, even his plants fell asleep.
One Sunday, his grandson Ranjid came to visit.
Ranjid looked at Mickel's cardigan, his slippers, and his crossword, and said:
"Grandpa... you're kind of... old."
Mickel dropped his pen.
Nobody had ever called him old.
Not even his doctor.
That night, Mickel couldn't sleep.
For the first time in forty years, he stayed up past 7 PM.
He took Ranjid's old phone and watched one video.
Then ten.
Then three hundred.
By sunrise, Mickel knew every trend, every dance, and every slang word on the internet.
He threw his cardigan out the window.
He sold his slippers.
And he bought a black outfit, a fanny pack, a red cap... and sunglasses.
The next morning, Ranjid came downstairs.
And there he was.
"Ranjid. From now on, you call me M.D."
Ranjid stared at him for five seconds.
"Like... a doctor?"
"No, Ranjid. Like a legend."
M.D. slowly put on his sunglasses and said:
"I'm not old. I'm vintage."
And that was the day the whole town changed forever.
Next time: M.D. goes to the gym.
