There was once a man named Rajesh Kumar, who had absolutely no responsibilities.
No job. No girlfriend. No hobbies.
Just Wi-Fi and unlimited screen time.
Every morning, Rajesh woke up at 2 PM, opened TikTok, and immediately lost three hours watching videos of people falling down stairs.
His mother would ask, "Rajesh, what are you doing with your life?"
And he would respond, "I'm building my aura, Mom."
Nobody knew what that meant.
One day, Rajesh decided he was going to become rich.
He watched one motivational video and immediately started calling himself a CEO.
His business?
Selling absolutely nothing.
He created a website, a logo, and a LinkedIn account.
His first employee was his cousin, who got paid in McDonald's.
After two weeks, Rajesh announced that his company was going bankrupt.
The investors were confused.
There were no investors.
His only customer was his mother, who had accidentally subscribed to his website.
But Rajesh didn't give up.
He looked into the camera and said:
"Every successful businessman has to start somewhere."
Then he opened TikTok...
And spent the next six hours watching brainrot.
His mother walked in, looked at him, and said:
"Your father was right. You are not the CEO."
Rajesh paused the video.
And for the first time in his life...
He actually got a job.
As a professional TikTok viewer.
Salary: zero dollars.
Hours: 24/7.
